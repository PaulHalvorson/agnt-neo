# Q&A Prep Sheet — EAIO Talk (5 minutes)

Weighting: 50% technical · 30% commercial · 20% client-dynamics. Rehearse at the
Wednesday dry run; answers are 30 seconds each, spoken plainly.

## Technical (expect 2–3 of these)

**"Why Claude over GPT/Gemini?"**
"I picked the platform that let me ship a governed agent fastest — skills, connectors
to Drive/Airtable/MailerLite, and rules the model actually follows. The method is
portable; the judgment layer is the asset. If your stack is different, the protocol
still runs."

**"How do you stop hallucination with client data?"**
"Architecture, not hope. The agent's rules forbid claiming access it doesn't have —
one stream is explicitly human-only and it must *ask* rather than guess. Fallback-first
design: assume the API fails, label every number with its source and freshness. And a
human is present for every run — that's in the skill file, not a vibe."

**"What breaks when you leave?"**
"Less than you'd think — that's the point of the engagement. The skill file IS the
handover: data streams by ID, rules, scorecard, voice guide. The client keeps the
work product by contract. What they lose is the judgment that wrote it — which is why
this is fractional-executive work, not automation-vendor work."

**"How long did the agent take to build?"**
"The first working version, about a day. The judgment encoded in it — exclusion
rules, flag carry-forward, the honesty rules — took weeks of running it with a real
client. That's the moat: production time, not build time."

## Commercial (expect 1–2)

**"What does an engagement like this cost?"**
"I price to the engagement, not the hour — a three-month transition engagement is a
five-figure commitment, scoped in tranches tied to board milestones. Happy to walk
through structure on a call." *(Never state ARC's actual fees — confidential.)*

**"What's in it for me if I refer you?"**
"A client who stops being your problem — I take the org-transition work you don't
want, and your client relationship stays yours. If you want a formal split, let's
talk structure on a call."

**"Are you selling us something today?"**
"No. Two gifts and one sentence of availability. If you never book a call, use the
prompt and the agent with my blessing."

## Client dynamics (expect 1 — and it's where you win the room)

**"How did a grieving community take to AI?"**
"They never met 'AI' — they met a report that was honest about what it knew and a
process that got their stories heard. The agent counts community voices, excludes
insiders from the numbers, and refuses to run unattended. Care, encoded as rules.
The tell: the campaign's guiding question isn't an open rate — it's 'is the community
we're saying goodbye to actually being heard?'"

**"Isn't automating an ending kind of cold?"**
"Automating the logistics is what makes the humanity possible. Every hour the agent
does the tallying is an hour the humans spend in Listening Circles. We automated the
counting, never the caring."
