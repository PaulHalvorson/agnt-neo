### The problem I take off your desk

Small businesses, nonprofits, and public agencies hit chapters nobody planned for: a
leader departs, a property sells, a program must wind down or scale suddenly. The
data is scattered, the story is emotional, and the board is exhausted. Most
consultants sell beginnings. **Almost nobody owns endings — and every organization
has one.**

### How I work: I · We · It

- **I — the leader.** Succession, decision rhythm, and the human work of letting go.
- **We — the community.** Listening infrastructure so stakeholders are genuinely
  heard — before decisions, not after.
- **It — the systems.** Forensic data reconstruction, communications and donation
  rails, and AI agents that carry the operational load with judgment encoded in
  their rules.
